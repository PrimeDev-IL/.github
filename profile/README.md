# Welcome to PrimeDev 🚀

**PrimeDev** is a boutique development studio founded by **Lior Lazar**.
We help startups and founders turn ideas into **working, deployed systems** — not just code, but solutions designed to operate in real-world environments.

Our DNA is rooted in **Security Operations & Automation (SOC / SOAR)**.
This means we think in **failure scenarios**, **monitoring**, and **operational clarity** from day one.
